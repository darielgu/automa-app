Automa

A macOS app that fills out job applications on your own machine. It reads the public internship and new-grad job boards, then drives a real browser through the application form while you watch.

Free, open source, and account-free. Your resume and answers stay in one folder on your Mac.

Download

  • Automa for Apple Silicon M1, M2, M3, M4 — every Mac since 2020
  • Automa for Intel Macs made before late 2020

Not sure which? Apple menu → About This Mac. If it says Apple M-anything, take the first one. All releases and checksums.

First launch takes one extra step

macOS will say “Automa” cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified. That is expected. The app is unsigned because an Apple Developer certificate costs $99 a year and this project is free. The whole source is published, so you can read exactly what it does.

  1. Drag Automa into your Applications folder.
  2. Right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.
  3. That is once. It opens normally from then on.

From the terminal instead: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Automa.app

To check the download first, compare shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Automa-mac-arm64.dmg against SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page.

What it does

  • Pulls the public SimplifyJobs internship and new-grad boards — about 32,000 listings — into a local database you can search instantly, online or not.
  • Fills the application in a real browser inside the app window, so you watch every field being filled.
  • Never answers a question about citizenship, sponsorship, disability, veteran status, race or gender with a model. It uses what you entered, or it stops and asks.
  • Records every run with the fields it filled, the value it used, and where that value came from. Confirmed applications land on a tracker board.

What actually works

About 59% of currently active listings are on an applicant tracking system Automa has a purpose-built adapter for. Having an adapter is not the same as working, so each one is graded by what it has been observed to do:

PlatformStateWhat that means
Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Work at a Startup Verified Each fills a complete application, resume upload included, against a practice application built from that platform's own markup.
Workday Partial Fills the My Information step. The rest runs behind an account on the employer's own tenant, and that is not verified here.
Everything else Generic Company career sites. Automa fills what it recognises and you finish.

Automa also refuses to claim a success it cannot prove. A submission is reported as confirmed only with real evidence: a confirmation page, a success URL, or the form demonstrably gone. Otherwise it says pending confirmation and leaves the judgement to you.

A look at it

Your data

There is no backend, no account and nothing to log in to. Your profile, resume and answers live in ~/Library/Application Support/Automa and are readable only by you. Listings come from a public JSON file on GitHub, fetched no more than once an hour. There is no analytics of any kind, on this page or in the app.

Requirements

  • macOS on Apple Silicon or Intel
  • About 400 MB of disk space
  • An internet connection to refresh the job list. Everything else works offline.
  • Optionally your own OpenAI or Anthropic API key for free-text answers. Automa works without one, using deterministic rules.
